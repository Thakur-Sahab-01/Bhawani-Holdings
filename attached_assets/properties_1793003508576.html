MAA BHAWANI PROPERTIES · MBP

Real Estate guidance, local connections

A brokerage-led real-estate service being developed in Banda, Uttar Pradesh, under the BHAWANI brand.

Our approach

We aim to connect real estate owners, buyers and local real estate professionals through real estate sourcing, listing promotion, buyer coordination and transaction support.

Initial service area

Banda, Uttar Pradesh, with nearby areas considered according to listing availability and verified local contacts.

Real Estate categories

Residential plots, houses and selected small commercial properties, subject to availability and appropriate due diligence.

Network-led service

MBP's planned model is to work with real estate owners and a network of independent local agents rather than buying real estate inventory for resale.

HOW WE WORK

From enquiry to informed decision

  1. Understand the buyer's or seller's requirement.
  2. Source potentially suitable properties through local contacts.
  3. Share available details and coordinate visits or discussions.
  4. Encourage independent title, land-use, ownership and document checks before any commitment.
  5. Clarify brokerage terms in writing before proceeding.

OUR PRINCIPLES

Transparency before transactions

  • No real estate is represented as available unless its availability is confirmed.
  • No title clearance, legal approval, return or appreciation is guaranteed.
  • Buyers and sellers should verify ownership, encumbrances, land use, approvals and applicable RERA status independently.
  • Any applicable agent registration and project marketing requirements must be met before regulated activities.

MBP's service details, commission arrangements and regulatory status should be confirmed in writing for each engagement.
